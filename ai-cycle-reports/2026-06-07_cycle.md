## Phase + timeline

- **Center of gravity:** late **Phase 2 → early Phase 3**. Capability race still funding a maximal build-out; deployment underway but ROI unproven at scale. The H100 price collapse, lengthening-but-cancelling power pipeline, and intensifying circular financing all say we are **later in the build than 6 months ago**.
- **Sentiment correction (sharp, ~15–35%, recoverable):** risk **elevated for 2026.** The ingredients are present *now* — record concentration, rising "disappointment" data, and debt scares already localized (Oracle −24% YTD reads like a pre-tremor). Could be triggered any quarter by one bad capex print or a marquee deployment failure.
- **Structural correction (deep, prolonged):** base case **~2027–2029 unchanged.** Needs the #1 trigger (capex guide-down) + accumulated ROI failure. Pushable to 2030+ if agent ROI inflects up (the 41%-payback figure is the thread to watch); pull-able into 2026 on a credit event in the circular-financing web.
- **Position for the sequence, not the date:** build-out → glut → washout → cheap infrastructure → value captured downstream. The H100 glut is the first visible instance of that sequence playing out one GPU-generation at a time.

## Survivor / casualty

**Likely survivors (consolidate through a downturn):**
- **Hyperscalers (MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN/META)** — other profit engines absorb the capex shock; buy distressed assets cheap.
- **TSMC** — makes everyone's chips; resets to sustainable level, doesn't go to zero.
- **NVDA** — dominant + clean balance sheet, but priced rich and *is* the capex; falls hardest in a sentiment air-pocket even as it survives.
- **Power/electrical (VST, GEV, ETN, NEE, nuclear/SMR)** — most durable tailwind; risk is demand-assumption cuts if builds cancel.

**Likely casualties (break in a washout):**
- **Leveraged single-customer infra (Oracle-style debt + OpenAI concentration; neocloud/SPV operators)** — the #5 Red names.
- **Thin-moat AI wrappers & seat-priced legacy SaaS** an agent can replicate.
- **Labor-arbitrage services** (BPO, call centers, low-end IT/content/translation).
- **Late infra entrants** building into the forming glut.
