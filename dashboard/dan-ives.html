Dan Ives Brain — 4th Industrial Revolution

Dan Ives (Wedbush Securities, Managing Director Tech Research) is Wall Street's loudest AI bull. His framework: every company in his coverage either monetizes AI directly (Microsoft Copilot, Apple Intelligence, Palantir federal AI) or enables the infrastructure (NVDA, cybersecurity). He covers primarily US large-cap tech (AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, META, AMZN), enterprise software (CRM, NOW, PLTR), cybersecurity (CRWD, PANW, ZS), and EV/autonomous (TSLA).
