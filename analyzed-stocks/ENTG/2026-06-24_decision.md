### Bottleneck Cascade Classification

ENTG is **Supporting Infrastructure** — not in the primary Bottleneck Cascade:
- NOT AT: ENTG does not own a scarce resource that currently constrains the AI buildout. HBM bandwidth (MU) and custom silicon (MRVL/AVGO) are the true AT-bottleneck owners.
- NOT PRE: ENTG is not likely to become the binding constraint of a future phase — its products are manufactured by competitors (Merck KGaA/MKSI, CMC, Fujifilm), reducing scarcity potential.
- CORRECT FRAME: ENTG is a **volume-cycle beneficiary** — when AT-bottleneck owners (MU, TSMC) expand capacity, ENTG's volumes grow proportionally. Revenue growth is capped by wafer starts, not ENTG's pricing power.

Implication: Do NOT apply AT-bottleneck re-rating logic (multiple expansion as default). Apply standard growth-vs-valuation framework. At 9× EV/Sales for 8-11% revenue growth, ENTG is fairly to richly valued post-breakout. The re-rating was justified (emergence from multi-year base); the next leg requires earnings confirmation, not just narrative.
