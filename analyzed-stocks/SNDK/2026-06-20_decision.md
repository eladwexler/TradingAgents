## Analysis Summary

**Identity:** SanDisk Corporation (SNDK, NASDAQ) — Technology / Computer Hardware.
Spun off from Western Digital in February 2025. Pure-play NAND flash memory and enterprise
SSD company operating through Kioxia JV (former Toshiba Memory partnership). No fabs owned;
buys NAND wafers under JV structure → minimal capex, enormous pricing leverage.

**The bull story:** SanDisk is a pure expression of the AI-driven NAND supercycle. NVIDIA's
Rubin inference architecture shifts memory from transient DRAM to persistent local storage,
structurally increasing NAND per AI server. Q1 2026 revenue of $5.95B (vs $1.69B a year ago)
and 78.3% gross margin signal the supply/demand imbalance is extreme. Goldman says the squeeze
runs to 2028. Apple's Tim Cook confirmed "unavoidable" memory price hikes as recently as June
2026. Forward P/E of 11.9x on $183 consensus EPS is not obviously expensive if the cycle
extends 2-3 more years. The balance sheet is pristine ($3.7B cash, effectively debt-free).

**The bear story:** 78% gross margins in a commodity business are a cycle-peak signal, not a
new paradigm. Historical precedent (NAND cycles of 2017-18, 2020-22) shows margins crash
40-60% within 12-24 months of peaks. Samsung and SK Hynix have every incentive to add
capacity at these prices. The analyst consensus (mean $1,751, median $1,632) already prices
in a 20-25% decline from current levels. At $2,184, the stock is above the upper Bollinger
Band, 23.9% above the 21 EMA, and at RSI 71 — stretched entry for new buyers. The stock
has already delivered the cycle return (+5,900%); the remaining risk/reward is asymmetric
to the downside.

**Key evidence:**
- Q1 2026 revenue $5.95B (97% YoY growth), gross margin 78.3%, FCF $2.99B
