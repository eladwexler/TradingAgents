## CRDO Bear Case — Ruthless Research Brief
**Date:** 2026-06-26 | **Current Price:** $268.03 | **Market Cap:** ~$50B

---

### THESIS: A Real Business Priced for a Scenario That Cannot Survive Contact With Reality

CRDO is not a fraud. It is a legitimate, fast-growing semiconductor company that has been priced as though the AI capex build-out never slows, NVIDIA never switches suppliers, hyperscalers never in-source, and interest rates never matter. All four of those assumptions are wrong simultaneously.

---

### 1. THE VALUATION IS MATHEMATICALLY INDEFENSIBLE AT CURRENT PRICE

**$50B market cap on $1.3B TTM revenue = 37x revenue.** The stock is priced at 30x *forward* P/E — but only if consensus EPS growth of +254% actually materializes. That is the single most dangerous number in this data bundle: the entire valuation edifice rests on a growth rate that has never been sustained by any semiconductor company past the initial hyperscaler adoption phase.

- Simply Wall St. DCF: **109% overvalued**. That is not a rounding error; it implies fair value around $128.
- The mean analyst target is **$269.81** — *fifty cents above the current price*. The consensus is already baked in. There is no room for error.
- Evercore's "$325 PT / market 40% too low" call was initiated **four days before the stock peaked at $308**. That PT has already been exceeded and retreated. The "40% too low" claim is an outlier view against 18 other analysts whose mean target implies 0.7% upside.

**Multiple compression math**: If growth slows from 157% YoY to the consensus 83% next year, then to 49% the year after, the growth-stock multiple re-rates toward 15-20x earnings. That alone — with *earnings growing* — produces a stock price flat to down 30% from here.

---

### 2. CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION IS AN EXISTENTIAL RISK, NOT A LINE-ITEM

The data explicitly states: *"One hyperscaler in-sourcing or switching to MRVL = revenue cliff."* This is the central asymmetry. CRDO's revenue tripled because *one or two hyperscalers* chose their SerDes. That is not a diversified business — it is a single-threaded bet.

- Microsoft, Google, and Amazon have all announced aggressive in-house silicon programs. Every custom ASIC effort they advance is a rack where CRDO's cable does not ship.
- The same Jensen keynote that bulls cite also **explicitly mentioned MARVELL in the same breath**: "the domain of copper... the core differentiator is electrical SerDes technology." MRVL has $1B+ in design wins, a full-stack portfolio, hyperscaler relationships going back a decade, and 10x CRDO's scale. Jensen names both. Customer procurement teams will dual-source.
- A 20% hyperscaler revenue shift to MRVL or in-house = $87M+ quarterly revenue impact = the entire prior-year quarter wiped out.

### 4. THE DUSTPHOTONICS ACQUISITION IS SPECULATIVE DEAD WEIGHT

Completed 5/28/26 — *four weeks ago.* Optical revenue will not appear until Q2 FY27 at earliest. The entire Evercore thesis ("market 40% too low on optical opportunity") is built on revenue that **does not exist yet** in any financial statement.

Silicon photonics integration is hard. The graveyard of silicon photonics acquisitions includes Intel's $345M Lightwire deal (failed to scale), Cisco's $475M Lightwire acquisition (marginalized), and dozens of startup failures. The assumption that CRDO can immediately monetize DustPhotonics' PIC technology into hyperscaler deployments is a venture-capital hope dressed as equity-analyst conviction.

If the optical ramp takes two quarters longer than modeled, the forward EPS numbers collapse, and the entire "40% too low" narrative reverses to "40% too high."

### 5. BASE RATES BRUTALIZE THIS SETUP

The unconditional P(beat SPY) over 12 months is **0.52** — a coin flip. The P25-P75 excess return band is **-17.2% to +36.1%**. That is not the return profile of a stock at 37x revenue.

The *downside* case from the base rate band: -17.2% excess to SPY. If SPY returns 10%, CRDO returns -7.2%. If SPY is flat, CRDO is at $249. If there is a macro shock and SPY falls 10%, CRDO at Beta 3.23 falls **32%** — to $182. That is not a tail scenario; that is beta arithmetic.

The stock already pulled back **13.4% in three trading days** from its ATH of $308.67 (6/22) to $268.03 (6/25). This on *no negative news*. With an ATR of $25.92 (~9.7%), a single bad earnings pre-announcement, a hyperscaler inventory build signal, or a rate hike surprise puts the stock at $220 in a week.

### 6. MACRO IS HOSTILE TO THIS EXACT STOCK

- CPI: **4.2%**. Warsh FOMC with 9/19 members penciling in a year-end rate hike.
- 10Y at **4.39%** and rising means duration risk is real. A 30x forward P/E stock is an extremely long-duration asset. Every 25bps rate hike compresses the multiple by 1-2 turns. Two hikes = 2-4 turns of compression on a 30x stock = **7-13% price decline from multiple alone**, before any earnings estimate cuts.
- Power constraints are delaying **50% of AI builds**. If the hyperscaler capex deployment timeline slips 2 quarters due to transformer lead times of 128 weeks to 4 years, CRDO's revenue ramp is pushed right. Consensus estimates do not price a 6-month delay in data center builds.

Q1 FY27: +157% YoY. Q4 FY26: +200%+ YoY.

These numbers are *mathematically impossible to sustain*. In Q1 FY28, CRDO will compare against a $437M quarter. To match 157% growth they would need $1.1B in a single quarter. When the growth rate "merely" comes in at +40% YoY instead of +83% consensus, the stock reprices as a mid-growth semiconductor company — and 40% growth semis trade at 15-20x forward earnings, not 30x. **That's a 33-50% multiple compression on growing earnings.**
