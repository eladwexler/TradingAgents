### 1. Survivable Bear-Case Drawdown

The proposal states a bear stop at $204 (-24%). That is **not the bear case — that is the stop.** The stated 12-month bear scenario is **$121 (-55%)**. At 1% portfolio weight, a 55% drawdown on the position costs 0.55% of the portfolio. That is survivable. At 2%, it costs 1.1% — also survivable, but uncomfortable alongside any correlated AI-infrastructure names already in the book.

**The critical sizing constraint is cross-position correlation, not just position beta alone.**

If the portfolio already holds MRVL, AVGO, NVDA, or any AI-infrastructure name with beta > 2, CRDO's beta 3.23 compounds portfolio drawdown in a risk-off tape in a non-linear way. In a sector rotation out of AI infrastructure, CRDO -55% and MRVL -35% happening simultaneously at 2% + 4% weights = **2.5% total portfolio hit in one move.** That is meaningful capital destruction, not a rounding error.

**My constraint:** 1% hard cap until after August earnings. No averaging up before Q2 FY27 print.

### 2. Insider Selling — This Is the Loudest Signal in the File

CEO + CFO + CTO + COO all selling on 10b5-1 plans **at prices within 15% of ATH** is not normal. 10b5-1 plans are set up months in advance, which means these plans were constructed when the stock was in the $150-$220 range — i.e., they pre-committed to selling at these levels before the stock ran. ### 3. Customer Concentration — The Unquantified Tail

The proposal names it as Risk #1 but does not quantify it. Let me:

CRDO's AEC/SerDes revenue is effectively single-customer (one hyperscaler). If that customer delays, pauses, or dual-sources aggressively for one quarter, revenue does not decelerate — it **gaps down**. The $437M/quarter run-rate was built on a specific deployment schedule. A single hyperscaler CapEx pause, purchasing freeze for one quarter, or qualification failure on a next-gen cable spec would not produce a smooth -20% revenue miss. It would produce a -40% to -60% miss with a guide-down.

At that point, gross margins compress (fixed costs against lower revenue), the multiple re-rates from 30x forward P/E to 12-15x on the new lower number, and you are looking at $100-$130 — **worse than the stated bear case.**

**The stated bear case of $121 may be optimistic if customer concentration crystallizes.** A true catastrophic bear case is $80-100 (70% drawdown from current).
