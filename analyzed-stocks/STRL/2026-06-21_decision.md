## Stage 2 — Research Debate

**Bull:** STRL is the cleanest pick-and-shovel for the physical AI build-out at a reasonable price. Before any GPU goes in, someone has to move the dirt, pour the foundations, and wire the power — Sterling does exactly that for hyperscale data centers and chip fabs, at 17% operating margins and 37% ROE. The CEC acquisition adds electrical, expanding wallet share. Backlog is growing in higher-complexity work. At 31.9x forward PE / PEG 1.47 with 100% FCF conversion, this is a high-quality compounder riding a multi-year capex wave.

**Bear:** It's a construction company trading at 77× trailing earnings after a 114% 90-day run and a 4× one-year move. Construction is brutally cyclical and margins on fixed-price work can reverse fast if input costs spike or a project goes sideways. The Current Ratio of 1.10 is thin for a company growing this fast — working capital could become a constraint. The entire forward-EPS doubling depends on data center capex staying at record levels; in a Phase 2→3 macro with valuation euphoria, a capex air pocket would compress both earnings and the multiple simultaneously.

**Manager:** The quality and backlog visibility outweigh the cyclicality concern over a 24-month horizon. The valuation is justified by growth + returns. **Overweight**, manage entry given the recent run.
