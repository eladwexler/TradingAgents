With a 45x forward P/E, any estimate cut hits the stock in two directions: lower earnings AND multiple compression.

CPO involves:

But if NVIDIA is co-promoting both MRVL (Computex June 2) and Coherent (June 16 blog), the optical "moat" is being shared. Multiple suppliers is NVIDIA's standard strategy to prevent any single supplier from extracting excessive rent.

**MRVL's CPO advantage may narrow faster than the bull case assumes** as Coherent, II-VI (now Coherent Corp), and InPhi-derived technologies ramp. The "optical monopoly" narrative is overstated.
