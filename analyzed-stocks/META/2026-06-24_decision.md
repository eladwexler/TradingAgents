**Bear case:** META's AI unit is in organizational chaos (Jordi's June 21 report: "gulag," executives leaving, CTO saying morale at worst ever). The $15B AI hiring was supposed to produce frontier models — it hasn't. If META fails to close the gap with OpenAI/Gemini on model capability, it becomes dependent on external models for its consumer products, paying away margin to AI providers. The capex treadmill ($125-145B guided 2026) is relentless and the ROI on data-center capex vs. ad revenue is difficult to verify. Price below all MAs with negative momentum means buying into a downtrend — technically risky. Jordi's explicit Cautious on hyperscaler spenders (and his "gulag" comment is from June 21, 2026 — very recent).

**Bull rebuttal:** The AI unit chaos affects LLAMA/AI lab aspirations, NOT the ad targeting ML that drives revenue. These are separate teams. The capex generates distribution infrastructure that reinforces the moat. And at PEG 0.81, the market is pricing in a lot of bad news already.

**Bear rebuttal:** Separate teams today, but strategy confusion bleeds into culture. And if OpenAI/Anthropic become dominant model providers, META's competitive position in the app layer weakens precisely when the application layer becomes the next bottleneck. Being a distributor with no proprietary model is weaker than being a distributor with proprietary models.

**Manager verdict:** Bull wins on valuation and FCF; bear wins on execution/momentum. Net: Overweight, not full BUY.
