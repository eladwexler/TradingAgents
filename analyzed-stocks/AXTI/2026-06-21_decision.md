## Stage 2 — Research Debate

**Bull:** AXT is a true bottleneck. There are only two scale producers of laser-grade (6N+ purity) InP substrate on Earth, and InP is physically irreplaceable for the lasers in 1.6T/6.4T optical modules. As AI data center interconnect speeds double every cycle, InP content per module rises. AXT has a $100M backlog (more than its trailing revenue) and 60%+ growth. This is the literal "picks and shovels of the picks and shovels." At $84 it's down 41% from highs — a gift on a structural winner.

**Bear:** Three killers. (1) Valuation: 57× sales, negative everything, 112× forward PE — you're paying for a perfect outcome. (2) China: the entire production base is Beijing Tongmei. AXT is squarely in the crossfire of US-China semiconductor restrictions — China has already restricted gallium/germanium exports, and InP could be next; conversely US customers may be wary of China-sourced material. (3) Technicals + sentiment: the chart just broke the 50 SMA on heavy volume while StockTwits is 80% bullish — the textbook setup for a crowded microcap unwinding. The $1.80 52-week low reminds you how violent this name can be on the downside.

**Manager:** The thesis is real but the price and the China risk are not adequately discounted; the technicals and euphoric sentiment argue against chasing. **Hold** (base), lean **Underweight** on the macro-adjusted view. Reserve any position for risk-tolerant capital sized as a lottery ticket.
