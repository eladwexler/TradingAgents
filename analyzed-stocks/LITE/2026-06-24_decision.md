## STAGE 2 — BOTTLENECK CASCADE CLASSIFICATION

**Classification: PRE→AT (Transitional — Optical Component Supplier at an Active Bottleneck)**

**Rationale:**

The optical interconnect node IS active in the Bottleneck Cascade. Hyperscaler capex ($725B/+77%) is creating real demand for AI interconnect; Goldman projects a 9× TAM expansion by 2028. However, LITE's position differs from a pure AT name like CRDO (which owns architectural IP in the Ethernet fabric) or MU (which owns the HBM constraint outright):

1. **What LITE makes:** Optical transceivers, VCSELs, 3D sensing chips, photonic integrated circuits (PICs), co-packaged optics (CPO), optical circuit switches. These are components that feed INTO the interconnect — input supplies to the bottleneck.

2. **Rent-duration:** LITE's rent depends on AI capex REMAINING high AND LITE winning design competitions for CPO against COHR, AAOI, and potentially hyperscaler in-house silicon photonics. Goldman's $154B TAM by 2028 is the ceiling; but at $842 and 46.5× forward PE, current price already embeds considerable rent-duration assumption.

3. **Rent-sustainability risk:** Three headwinds: (a) commoditization — optical transceivers historically compress over 18-24 months post-shortage; (b) customer concentration — Apple 3D sensing/VCSEL revenue remains a swing factor if iPhone units disappoint; (c) the NVIDIA $2B partnership is real but does NOT guarantee exclusive CPO supply — it signals NVIDIA is investing in the ecosystem, not locking LITE in.

4. **PRE→AT vs. AT:** LITE does NOT own a constraint that rivals cannot bypass. COHR (formerly II-VI/Coherent) is a direct competing CPO supplier also Overweight at JPM, with similar backlog claims. Hyperscalers building in-house silicon photonics (as Meta announced for custom AI silicon) is the 24–36 month risk that could commoditize the entire merchant optical sector.

**Under the PRE→AT frame:** Multiple expansion is possible IF LITE wins the CPO race decisively (becomes the Kleenex of co-packaged optics). At current price, the market is partially pricing this. The bear case is multiple compression back to 20–25× forward earnings as the competition sharpens and commodity optics pricing resumes.
