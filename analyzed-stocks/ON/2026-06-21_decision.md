**Secular driver:** Power is the binding AI constraint, and AI data centers need efficient power conversion at every stage (grid → rack → chip). GaN and SiC power devices improve efficiency and thermal performance — onsemi is a scaled supplier. Simultaneously, physical AI / robotics (humanoids, automation) need power management and sensing, where onsemi has IP. This diversifies ON beyond its cyclical auto base into structural AI-power demand.

**Risk:** Auto/industrial is onsemi's core today and it's in a cyclical trough (soft EV demand, weak GM). The AI data center segment, while fast-growing, is still small relative to auto. Revenue growth is modest (+8% current FY) precisely because the auto drag offsets the data center ramp. A prolonged auto downturn caps the thesis near-term.

## Stage 2 — Research Debate

**Bull:** onsemi is a cheap (PEG 0.40), profitable, FCF-generative power-semi leader transitioning from cyclical auto into structural AI-data-center power and physical AI. The GaNEXUS launch directly addresses AI data center and robotics power — the fastest-growing power-electronics demand pool. You're buying a real franchise with $1.28B FCF at 28x forward earnings into the bottom of an auto cycle — exactly when cyclicals should be accumulated.

**Bear:** It's still fundamentally an auto/industrial semi, and that market is "doing horrible" (GM weak, EV demand soft) — that's most of the revenue. The data center segment is exciting but small; it can't offset the auto drag yet, which is why revenue growth is only +8%. The stock is near its 52-week high while the *27-analyst consensus target is 12% below spot*. The trailing PE of 89x reflects how depressed earnings are. If the auto recovery is delayed, the forward EPS of $4.32 is a fantasy and the stock de-rates toward analyst fair value (~$105) or lower.

**Manager:** The PEG 0.40 + FCF + physical-AI pivot make this attractive over a 24-36mo horizon despite the auto trough — cyclicals are best bought at the bottom of the cycle, not the top. But the near-high entry and below-spot analyst targets argue for buying on weakness. **Overweight**, sized moderately, add on pullbacks.
