**Secular driver:** Long-duration energy storage (LDES) is a critical piece of the AI-power equation. AI data centers need firm, 24/7 power; intermittent renewables require storage to firm them; the grid needs storage to handle data center load spikes. Eos's zinc chemistry is non-flammable, lithium-free, domestically sourced, and suited to 4–12 hour durations — a differentiated niche. This sits inside the same "power is the binding constraint" thesis as nuclear (OKLO/SMR), grid (GEV/ETN), and uranium (CCJ).

**Risk:** (a) Profitability — Eos has never been sustainably profitable; scaling manufacturing is capital-intensive and execution-heavy. (b) Dilution/financing — heavy cash burn (-$282M FCF) means more capital raises likely. (c) Competition — LDES is crowded (flow batteries, iron-air/Form Energy, lithium LFP at scale). (d) The DOE loan/policy environment under a new administration is uncertain.
